# Handoff

Updated: 2026-10-17

## What this is

One page, `index.html`, plus photos in `images/`. Open the file in a browser. No build step. Repo: https://github.com/Elcid000/house-tank

A Saturday job sheet for a house tank and booster pump. City water is too weak for the showers, so it fills the tank and the pump pushes it to the faucets. When the pump is off it is a closed door, so a backup pipe skips the pump.

Checkboxes and the height/pressure calculator save in the browser (`localStorage`).

## Tabs

- **Plan** — why the tank, the two taps, reach calculator, six gates before the meter closes
- **Buy** — sizes, what to say at the counter, checklist with a photo of each part
- **Day** — clock blocks that start at 11:30, roles, store, go or no-go
- **Build** — water path on one schematic, order of work, valve positions
- **Safety**
- **Trouble**
